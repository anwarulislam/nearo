The picture brief was a three-by-three photographic contact sheet from a single architectural walk, with the nine subjects listed above. The built-in image-generation tool supplied the sheet, and each panel was cropped into its own JPEG.

To inspect the same sample devices locally, launch a Debug build with `scripts/dev.sh -NearoDemoData`. For full-size screenshots and their generation steps, see the [capture guide](captures.md).
